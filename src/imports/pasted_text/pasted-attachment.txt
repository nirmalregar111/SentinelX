# FEATURE

When a person enters a restricted area and the existing SentinelX intrusion engine confirms the intrusion:

```text
PERSON DETECTED
        ↓
PERSON ENTERS RESTRICTED ZONE
        ↓
EXISTING INTRUSION CONFIRMATION
        ↓
INCIDENT CREATED
        ↓
VIDEO EVIDENCE CREATED
        ↓
```

The email must be associated with the same incident.

Do NOT trigger an email for simple person detection.

Only send it when the existing intrusion logic confirms a restricted-area violation.

# 10. IMPORTANT EMAIL LOGIC

The final implementation should follow:

```text
Person detected
      ↓
Is person inside restricted zone?
      ↓
YES
      ↓
Existing multi-frame confirmation
      ↓
Intrusion confirmed
      ↓
Create incident
      ↓
Create video evidence
      ↓
Send email
      ↓
Record email status
```

Do NOT send an email every frame.

Do NOT send repeated emails while the same person remains inside the restricted zone.

For one continuous intrusion:

```text
1 Incident
1 Evidence
1 Email Alert
```

If the person leaves and later enters again, treat it as a new intrusion according to the existing intrusion logic and allow a new alert.
